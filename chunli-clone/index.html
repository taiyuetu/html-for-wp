We Offer Various Tea Products

Zhejiang Chunli Tea Co., Ltd. is a leading tea export company with an impressive volume of 35,000 tons annually. Our client base extends to most African and Central Asian countries, including Algeria, Morocco, Mali, Niger, Libya, Mauritania, Senegal, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan as well as Russia, Europe, and the United States. Our company is renowned for high-quality tea production and has won the appreciation and loyalty of clients due to our strong production capacity and strict quality control measures.

More About Us

CHUNLI Tea is committed to providing high-quality tea products and promoting healthy living. With over a decade of experience in tea production and export, we have developed a reliable and efficient supply chain to deliver the best products to customers in different parts of the world. Our carefully selected raw materials and advanced production technology ensure that our tea is highly nutritious, safe, and of excellent quality. you can be sure that you are getting top tea products the industry has to offer.

More About Us

CHUNLI Tea Company has strict quality control measures including multiple inspections and tests at every stage of production, ensuring that our tea is of consistently high quality. This has earned us a well-deserved reputation for excellence in the industry, and our customers have rewarded us with a high return customer rate.
Our products are highly sought-after in many countries, and we take great pride in seeing our tea sold in different parts of the world.

More About Us

CHUNLI Tea Company has the most advanced automatic tea production line. Workers can easily control the entire production process through the digital production line computer control system, making the entire process intelligent, continuous, standardized and clean.
