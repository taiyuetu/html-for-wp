About NORDAX

From a single machining workshop in Ningbo to a global wheel-end manufacturing group — built on precision, verified at every step.

20+ Years of
Engineering

Our Story

Precision is a habit, not a feature.

NORDAX began in 2006 as a twelve-person machining workshop serving the Ningbo automotive cluster. The founding rule was simple: control every process in-house, and measure everything twice.

Today the group operates seven production plants, an in-house forging division and three distribution centers across three continents. More than 1,200 people — including 120 engineers — support OE programs and professional aftermarket distributors in over 80 countries.

The rule has not changed. From raw bar stock to boxed product, nothing leaves a NORDAX plant without a measurement, a record and a signature.

Milestones

Two decades of compounding precision.

2006

Founded in Ningbo

Twelve machinists, one CNC shop, and the first contracts supplying hub flanges to regional Tier-1 suppliers.

2010

First export program

Complete hub assemblies enter the European aftermarket under the NORDAX brand for the first time.

2014

What Drives Us

Three principles, no exceptions.

01

Precision First

If a dimension matters to the vehicle, it matters to us. Tolerances are engineered, measured and archived — not promised in a catalog.

02

Vertical Integration

Forging, machining, heat treatment, assembly and testing under one group — so quality never depends on someone else’s schedule.

03

Long-Term Partnership

We build programs, not transactions: protected allocations, stable pricing and engineering support that outlasts any single order.
