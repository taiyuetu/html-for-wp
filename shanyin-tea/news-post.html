Field note 30 September 2026 3 min

Off-season on the north slope.

The terraces are resting. What the mountain does between Octobers — and why a cold winter is a good vintage announcement.

By late September the picking is a memory and the terraces belong to the mist again. The work that remains is slow: terrace walls to mend before the winter freeze, channels to clear, and the pruning that decides next April's buds.

The leaf does not stop, it only slows. Below eight degrees the bush shuts down and spends the winter counting its reserves. A cold winter is a good vintage announcement — the bud that struggles wakes up concentrated.

In the village the woks are re-seasoned and the drying room is quiet. We taste last spring against the spring before it, and start the argument about which lots get the best of next year's windows. The mountain keeps its own time. We keep its calendar.
