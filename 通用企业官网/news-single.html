NORDAX Commissions Third Automated Wheel-End Line in Ningbo

Company NewsMarch 12, 20264 min read

The new line adds 2.4 million hub units of annual capacity, dedicated to European OE and Tier-1 programs — and makes traceability a standard, not an option.

NORDAX Industrial Group today commissioned its third fully automated wheel-end production line at the Ningbo Advanced Manufacturing Campus. The line is dedicated to Gen-3 hub assemblies for European OE and Tier-1 programs, raising group capacity for the platform to more than 8 million units per year.

The €18 million investment links forging, machining, assembly and end-of-line testing into a single digital flow. Changeovers between reference families take under 20 minutes, allowing NORDAX to run OE-grade batches alongside aftermarket volumes without the usual lead-time penalty.

Built for OE traceability

Every hub unit leaving the line carries a laser-etched data matrix code linking it to its heat number, machining batch and test results. Distributors can request the full digital passport for any serial number — a capability previously reserved for OE contracts.

“Our customers are not buying bearings — they are buying certainty. This line gives every hub unit a digital passport, from heat number to end-of-line noise signature.” Daniel Wu — Chief Operating Officer, NORDAX Industrial Group
  • 2.4 million units of additional annual capacity
  • 38 inline inspection points, 100% torque and noise testing
  • Under 20-minute changeover between reference families
  • Cutting-fluid consumption reduced by 40% versus the 2019 line

What it means for distributors

For the aftermarket, the line removes the traditional trade-off between OE-grade quality and aftermarket availability. High-runner references produced on the line will be stocked in all three global warehouses, with same-week dispatch as standard.

The first OE shipments leave the campus in April 2026. Aftermarket stock of the covered references is expected to reach Hamburg, Atlanta and Ningbo warehouses by June 2026. Distributors can pre-book allocations through their regional sales managers.
