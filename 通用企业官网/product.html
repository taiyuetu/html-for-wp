The NX-512410 is a third-generation wheel hub unit designed for demanding passenger-car applications. The flange, sensor ring and bearing are integrated into a single sealed assembly that ships ready to install — no pressing, no adjustment, no greasing.

Every unit is manufactured from GCr15 bearing steel, induction hardened and finished to OE surface specifications. End-of-line testing covers torque, preload and noise signature so that warranty exposure stays where it belongs — with us.

  • Integrated magnetic encoder ring compatible with factory ABS systems
  • Triple-lip seal resists water, salt and bearing contamination
  • Pre-set bearing preload — torque to specification and go
  • Laser-etched traceability code on every flange
