Our Story

20+ Years of Bearing Excellence

Founded in 2004 in the heart of China's bearing industrial zone in Zhejiang Province, Apex Bearing Technologies began as a small workshop with a singular mission: to manufacture wheel hub bearings that match or exceed OE specifications at accessible prices.

Over two decades, we've grown into a 50,000 m² state-of-the-art manufacturing facility with 200+ skilled employees, 6 automated production lines, and an in-house R&D center. Today, we export to over 80 countries across six continents, serving distributors, importers, automotive parts chains, and OEM buyers worldwide.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Promise

Our Mission

To deliver precision-engineered wheel hub bearings that empower global automotive aftermarket distributors with OE-quality products at competitive prices, backed by exceptional service and technical support.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted wheel hub bearing manufacturer — the first choice for automotive distributors seeking reliability, innovation, and partnership in every market we serve.

Our Promise

Every bearing that leaves our factory is 100% inspected. We stand behind our products with full warranty, technical documentation, and a commitment to zero-defect delivery.

Our Journey

Company History

2004

Company Founded

Apex Bearing established in Zhejiang bearing industrial zone with initial focus on tapered roller bearings for commercial vehicles.

2008

ISO 9001 Certified

Achieved ISO 9001:2008 certification. Expanded product range to include wheel hub assemblies for passenger vehicles.

2012

First Export Markets

Began exporting to Southeast Asia and Middle East. Established first international distribution partnerships.

2016

IATF 16949 Certified

Achieved IATF 16949 automotive quality management certification. Opened new 30,000 m² production facility.

2020

4,000 SKU Milestone

Reached 4,000+ SKU catalog covering 99% of passenger vehicles. Expanded into European and African markets.

2024

EV & Global Expansion

Launched dedicated EV hub bearing line. Expanded to 80+ export countries. 50,000 m² total facility with 6 automated lines.
