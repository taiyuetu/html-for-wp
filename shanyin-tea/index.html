01 Origin 山场

Cloud first,
then stone,
then leaf.

Huangshan is not a gentle mountain. Granite shoulders, sixty per cent of days under cloud, a soil that stays cold into May. The tea that grows here is slow — and it tastes of the delay.

Our gardens sit between 760 and 840 metres on north-facing slopes: the side that holds moisture, loses the light, and ripens a leaf a full week after the valley below has finished picking.

02 Harvest 采摘

Four days a year.
Two hundred pairs
of hands.

Picking begins when the fog lifts and stops when the leaf warms. Between six and ten in the morning, nothing else happens on this slope.

A picker gathers three to four kilos of fresh leaf in a morning. It takes forty thousand buds to make one kilo of finished tea — which is why we do not sell it by the case.

03 Craft 制茶

The leaf decides.
We only follow.

Three moves, a hundred and ten years of habit, and a great deal of restraint.

i

Wither 摊放

Six hours on bamboo, one leaf deep. The leaf loses a third of its water and stops smelling of grass.

ii

Fix 杀青

A hand-turned wok at 220 °C. Ninety seconds to stop the oxidation — no more, or the orchid note turns to toast.

iii

Shape 揉捻

Rolled, pressed, dried twice. The final firing is judged by sound, not by the clock.

05 Ritual 冲泡

Three grams.
Eighty degrees.
Ninety seconds.

Green tea is not brewed, it is interrupted. Water below boiling, a vessel that holds heat gently, and a second infusion you will prefer to the first.

  1. 03 gleaf per 150 ml
  2. 80 °Cspring water, never boiling
  3. 90 sfirst infusion, no lid
  4. ×3the leaf will keep giving

Morning, before the house is awake. The first infusion is for the room; the second is for you.

    Certified since 2019. Certificate and transaction document supplied with each shipment.

    A QR on every box resolves to the terrace, the picking team, and the morning it was cut.
