Premium Bearing Steel

Vacuum-degassed GCr15 (SAE 52100) steel with ultra-low oxygen content for fatigue life exceeding 107 cycles at rated load.

Low Noise & Vibration

Precision-ground raceways with Ra ≤ 0.08 μm surface finish. 100% NVH tested to V3/V4 classification for ultra-quiet operation.

Advanced Sealing

Triple-lip NBR contact seal with stainless steel slinger prevents water, dust, and contaminant ingress. IP67-equivalent protection.

Long Service Life

Optimized internal geometry and high-temperature grease formulation ensure consistent performance through 80,000+ km in normal driving conditions.

Precision Fit

CNC machined to ±0.001mm tolerance. Direct drop-in replacement — no modifications required. Pre-lubricated and ready to install.

Full Traceability

Each bearing is laser-etched with a unique serial number. Complete batch traceability from raw steel to finished product with digital QC records.
