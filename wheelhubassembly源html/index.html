Quality Assurance

Rigorous Quality Control Process

Every bearing undergoes 6-stage inspection before global shipment

01

Raw Material Inspection

100% incoming inspection of bearing-grade steel (GCr15/SAE 52100). Spectrometer analysis and hardness testing for every batch.

02

Precision Machining

CNC grinding with ±0.001mm tolerance. Multi-axis turning centers with in-process gauging and automatic tool compensation.

03

Automated Assembly

Clean-room assembly environment. Automated grease filling, seal installation, and preload setting for consistent quality.

04

Performance Testing

100% noise & vibration testing. Endurance testing at rated load. Water/dust seal integrity verification per IP standards.

05

Final Packaging

Anti-corrosion treatment. Branded or neutral packaging options. Each unit individually sealed with full traceability label.

06

Global Shipment

FCL/LCL container loading. Major port access within 2 hours. Complete export documentation with certificate of origin.
