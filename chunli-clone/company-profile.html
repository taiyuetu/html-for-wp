Company Profile

ABOUT CHUNLI

Our goal is our clients’ success

CHUNLI TEA Co., Ltd was set up in 1988 in Zhejiang Province China. Its predecessor is CHUNFENG Tea Factory, which is located besides our 2500+ hectare high mountain tea garden. CHUNLI are famous for tea production, processing and export. We can control product quality from end to end.

As the company grows, CHUNLI now has more than 10000 m2 office building and 70000 m2 manufacturing plant. Our goal is our customers’ success. We aim to supply good quality tea to our customers all over the world with reasonable price.

CHUNLI has its own tea processing factory and stable supply chain. The exporting quantity of our products reaches 40000 tons per year, and it’s still rising steadily.

CHUNLI provides a variety of premium and organic tea. Our range of green teas is constantly growing. We currently offer our customers over 500 types of green tea.

Holding a trade license, CHUNLI exports products to Africa, Europe, Central Asia and Middle-east area. Our products gain wide praise at home and abroad.

CHUNLI is an innovative and technological self-export enterprise, specializing in research, development, production, and sales of tea products. The company has been awarded the title of National High-tech Enterprise, it has 12 domestic patents, and has obtained ISO9001 Quality Management System Certification, ISO22000 Food Safety Management System Certification, and US FDA Access Certification. According to the needs of international customers, our company also applied for International Halal Certification, Nigeria NAFDAC Access Certification, and etc. CHUNLI established a complete raw material traceability system and laboratory monitoring system. It is our duty to ensure the tea quality and satisfy customers’ requirements.

CHUNLI enjoys convenient access to major transportation networks. We warmly welcome customers from all over the world to visit our factory and negotiate business with us based on mutual benefit.
