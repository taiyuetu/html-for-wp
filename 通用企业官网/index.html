About NORDAX

A global manufacturer built on precision.

Founded in 2006, NORDAX Industrial Group has grown from a single machining workshop in Ningbo into a global manufacturer of wheel-end and drivetrain components. Today, seven production plants and more than 1,200 employees support customers in over 80 countries.

From raw-material forging to end-of-line testing, every process is controlled in-house — allowing us to guarantee the dimensional accuracy, material integrity and full traceability that OE programs and professional aftermarket distributors demand.
