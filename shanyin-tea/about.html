About · 关于 · The people of the garden

Two families, one mountain.

SHANYIN is three terraces on the north slopes of Huangshan, worked by two families and two hundred pairs of hands. The name means mountain-hidden — which is where the tea comes from, and where it stays.

01 Beginnings 起源

One slope, kept
in the family.

The Li family has picked these terraces for three generations — first for the village cooperative, then for whoever paid the most that spring. In 2019 we stopped selling the leaf into blends and put our own name on the tin. SHANYIN, mountain-hidden.

Two neighbouring families joined with their slopes; the woks stayed where they were. The first container left Ningbo in 2021. Today the tea goes, quietly, to twenty-one countries — most of it to tea houses that pour it within the year it was picked.

Nothing about that scale has changed the slope. The pick is still four days a year. The woks are still turned by hand. The mountain keeps its own time, and we keep its calendar.

02 Principles 原则

What we will
not do.

Five refusals, kept since the first tin. They cost us volume every spring, and they are the reason the lots sell out.

  1. i

    Nothing blended.

    One terrace, one lot, one number. A tea that says Maofeng is Maofeng from our slope, or it is not ours.

  2. ii

    Nothing held over.

    Last spring's leaf is last spring's. When a lot sells out, it stays sold out until the mountain says otherwise.

  3. iii

    Nothing hidden.

    The papers travel with the tea — certificates, the 486-parameter panel, the terrace it came from. Failures included.

  4. iv

    No shortcuts in the wok.

    Ninety seconds at 220 °C, turned by hand. A machine would be faster, and it would not be this tea.

  5. v

    The village eats first.

    Rejected lots go to the local market, unlabelled. The people who pick the tea drink it too.

03 The people 人

Two hundred pairs
of hands.

The same picking teams return every April — some of them for the whole four days, all of them for the whole slope.

Head of making

Li Wenshan

Second generation at the wok. Judges the final firing by sound, not by the clock — and has never once been early to dinner in April.

Export & lots

Xu Lan

Keeps the papers honest and the samples on the plane. Twenty-one countries, one spreadsheet, no exceptions to the lot number.

Terrace manager

Chen Yuqi

Walks the north slope first every morning of the year, and decides when the pick begins. Has overruled the calendar twice and been right both times.

Visit 来访

Pour it, or come
and see it.

The garden receives visitors by appointment, April to October. Write ahead, wear shoes you do not mind losing to the mud, and allow a morning — the fog does not lift for anyone in a hurry.
