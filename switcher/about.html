The Company

About AXIOM

A manufacturer of architectural switching infrastructure — developed, tooled, assembled and finished in one plant, specified by reference in 64 countries.

FOUNDED 1978 · MEINERZHAGEN
640 PEOPLE · 38 OFFICES

01

Statement

One plant. One standard. Every wall.

Vertical integration is not a strategy here. It is the precondition for batch control.

AXIOM was founded in 1978 as an electrical workshop and has never outsourced a core process since. Die-casting, tooling, contact assembly, finishing and batch archiving sit under one roof in Meinerzhagen — because a finish promised for 2026 must still be orderable in 2036.

The company builds one mechanical platform — 55 mm fixing centres, a Ø 60 mm back box, a single-screw carrier — and resolves every product decision against the wall plane: flush into plaster, recessed into stone, gasketed onto concrete. Architectural credibility is checked at detail level, in writing.

02

Principles

Four rules the platform does not break.

Product decisions are checked against these before they reach a drawing.

01

Modularity first

The insert is the product; the plate is the architecture. Function changes never touch the wall finish — a specification survives its third re-planning.

02

Precision as documentation

Every dimension is drawn, every tolerance stated, every batch archived. If it is not written down, it does not ship.

03

One plant

Tooling, die-casting, assembly and finishing under one roof. Substitutions are decided by engineers, not by freight schedules.

04

Longevity

Five-year finish re-order guarantee as standard, parts availability measured in decades, and a batch archive that outlives the building phase.

03

History

Forty-eight years, one platform.

Milestones are mechanical, not marketing.

  1. 1978

    Founded

    Two-man electrical workshop in Meinerzhagen; first switch program for local joineries.

  2. 1985

    Modular platform

    55 mm carrier with single-screw mounting — the geometry every AXIOM family still uses.

  3. 1994

    FLUSH ZERO principle

    Patent for frameless skim-coat integration; the plate becomes part of the plaster build-up.

  4. 2003

    STONE LINE

    First genuine-stone fronts on bonded composite; quarry-lot archiving begins.

  5. 2012

    AX-BUS

    Local-first control topology launched — scenes on the line, cloud optional.

  6. 2018

    Accredited laboratory

    In-house testing lab accredited; ENEC+ routine testing moves on-site.

  7. 2024

    Digital batch archive

    Every finish batch since 2016 queryable by project code; 480 BIM objects published.

04

Production

Two lines. One archive.

Capacity is allocated to specification business first.

4.2 million inserts per year leave the two assembly lines — each one logged to a batch, each batch logged to a project code.

The finish lab holds the archive: every anodize bath, lacquer mix and quarry lot since 2016, spectrally measured. A re-order is matched before dispatch — deviation beyond ΔE 1.5 triggers re-finishing, not shipping.
