PRICE ON SPECIFICATION · MOQ 10 UNITS · LEAD TIME 2 WEEKS

Function lives in the insert, not the plate. The switch insert carries a snap-action contact in silver alloy on a glass-fibre carrier, clipped into the standard 55 mm unit and terminated with 0.5 N·m torque terminals.

  • Interchangeable inserts. Switch, dimmer, socket and data share one carrier geometry across all families.
  • 55 mm platform. Ø 60 mm machine back box, single-screw carrier, 32 mm minimum depth.
  • Tool-free mounting. Insert and rocker clip in without tools; plates mount screw-less.
  • Batch-controlled finishes. Every finish coded, archived and re-orderable for five years.

  1. 01

    Verify the back box

    Ø 60 mm machine box, 55 mm fixing centres, 32 mm minimum depth. For FLUSH ZERO, coordinate the box set with the skim-coat build-up.

  2. 02

    Terminate the conductors

    2 × 1.5–2.5 mm² on the insert terminals, torque 0.5 N·m. Through-wiring continues on the terminal pair.

  3. 03

    Clip insert and rocker

    Insert clips onto the carrier tool-free; the rocker snaps onto the insert axis in one movement.

  4. 04

    Mount the plate

    Screw-less plate clips on flush; align the shadow gap evenly at 2 mm on all sides.
