Chunmee Green Tea — 41022 Series

Green · Pan-fired. The flagship bulk tea of our export programme — twelve commercial grades produced to a locked specification for blending and packing houses.

Green · Pan-Fired

Specification overview

Tightly rolled, uniform eyebrow leaf with a brisk, clean-astringent cup and bright yellow-green liquor. Produced from spring and autumn flushes at our Huangshan and Xiuning bases, blended to target spec and released only after laboratory clearance.

  • Blend-friendly profile for North & West African programmes
  • Batch-to-batch consistency, lot-coded and traceable
  • Residue-screened against full EU MRL lists
  • Private-label packing available from 500 kg

Quality & Compliance

Pesticide residues compliant with EU MRL / USDA NOP; heavy metals and microbiology per batch. HACCP and ISO 22000 certified processing.

Packing & Loading

5-ply kraft-paper bags, 25–30 kg net; ~26 MT per 40' HQ palletised or loose. Jumbo bags and private-label retail packing on request.

Shipping Documents

Commercial invoice & packing list, CO / Form A / EUR.1, phytosanitary & fumigation certificates, COA, third-party SGS report on request.
