For The First Time in The Yangtze River Delta, This Special Train Departs Today! Towards The Largest City in Central Asia...

View(s)

Leave a message

Exciting!

"Chunli Train - Shengzhou Tea Special Train"

It just drove away~

Shengzhou green tea "drove" country's door

Go to Central Asia!

Thanks to CCTV News, China News Network, Zhejiang Daily, Toutiao Today, Shengzhou News, Shengzhou Live, Shaoxing Evening News and many other mainstream media reports.

Today (June 8) morning, with the sound of siren, a China-Europe (Yixinou) train carrying 100 TEUs of Shengzhou green tea, "Chunli-Shengzhou Tea Special Train", set off from Yiwu towards The largest city in Central Asia-Tashkent, the capital of Uzbekistan, drove away.

Chinese word:
China-Europe Train
Departure Ceremony of Chunli
(Yiwu - Tashkent)
2021, June, 8th

◆Zhou Jianhua

Zhejiang Provincial Department of Commerce
Foreign Trade Service Center
Director:

Shengzhou Green Tea goes abroad in the form of a whole train. This is not only the first time for Chunli, but also the first time for Zhejiang Green Tea. It is also the first special tea train in the Yangtze River Delta. This is bound to expand overseas markets for Shengzhou tea and serve Zhejiang The development of provincial agricultural products has played a strong role in promoting.

In 2020, the country's green tea exports were 293,000 tons, accounting for 84.1% of the total tea exports, while Shengzhou was in a leading position in the country with nearly 60,000 tons of pearl tea exports, and it was a veritable hometown of tea.

Chinese word:
Chunli good tea, makes the world fragrant.

As one of the main tea export enterprises in Shengzhou, Chunli Tea Industry has been committed to introducing to the world high-quality and good-quality green tea with low pesticide residues and good taste. After years of hard work, it has established a strong presence in Central Asia, the European Union, North Africa and other regions. Brand influence.

Chinese word:
There is a tea factory in Zhejiang called Chunli, who tells the story of Chunli and tastes good tea.

However, since last year, due to the impact of the global epidemic, land, sea, and air transportation have been blocked to varying degrees, and the export volume of Chinese green tea has fluctuated, and the pace of Shengzhou green tea going abroad has also been affected. Under the new situation, how to turn crises into opportunities and go against the trend? The official launch of the "Belt and Road" China-Europe Express has brought inspiration and hope to enterprises.

Chinese word:
Congratulations to Zhejiang Chunli Tea Co., Ltd. · Chunli - Shengzhou Green Tea Train on the smooth departure.

Chinese Words:
China - Europe (Yiwu - Tashkent)
Chunli
Shengzhou Green Tea Special Train

Looking at the far away train, Huang Chunjun, chairman of Zhejiang Chunli Tea Industry Co., Ltd., described his feelings as "just like a daughter marrying away".

◆Huang Chunjun

Zhejiang Chunli Tea Industry
Chairman of the Board

This is the first time that we have cooperated with Yixinou, named Chunli Company, and it is also the first train in Zhejiang Province. We must continuously deliver Chunli tea to their market, and let Chunli tea become more and more popular. They also imported our tea culture into Uzbekistan.

Huang Chunjun, chairman of Zhejiang Chunli Tea Industry Co., Ltd., said that the "Belt and Road" China Railway Express has brought him inspiration and hope. "The China-Europe Express Express has short shipping time, low cost, large capacity, zero contact, economical and environmental protection, safety and efficiency, which can effectively help our tea go out and actively expand overseas markets."

Chinese words:
Special Tea Train

Up to now, the area of 180,000 mu of tea gardens in Shengzhou has reached 105,000 mu of improved varieties of tea gardens, and a traceability management system has been introduced in the export of tea. With the increasing influence of Shengzhou green tea in the world, the International Tea Committee also awarded Shengzhou the title of "World Famous Tea Village".

Chunli Company, will export high-quality green tea to the world all the time.
